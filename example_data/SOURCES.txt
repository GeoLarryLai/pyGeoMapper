# pyGeoMapper Example Data Provenance & Public Licenses

Study Area:
  2014 Oso (SR 530) Landslide & North Fork Stillaguamish River Valley, Washington State, USA
  Bounding Box (WGS84): [-121.860, 48.268, -121.834, 48.288]
  Projected Grid CRS: EPSG:32610 (WGS 84 / UTM Zone 10N), 200 x 200 pixels (~10 m grid spacing)

1. `oso_dem_pre_cop30.tif` (Pre-Landslide Digital Elevation Model, 2011-2013)
   - Source: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (`Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N48_00_W122_00_DEM`) via AWS Open Data /
     Element84 Earth Search STAC API (`https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1`, collection `cop-dem-glo-30`)
   - S3 Asset: `s3://copernicus-dem-30m/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N48_00_W122_00_DEM/Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N48_00_DEM.tif`
   - Acquisition Epoch: 2011-2013 TanDEM-X radar interferometry (prior to the March 22, 2014 Oso Landslide)
   - License: Copernicus DEM Open / Free & Open Access License (European Union / ESA)

2. `oso_dem_post_3dep.tif` (Post-Landslide Digital Elevation Model, post-2014 LiDAR)
   - Source: U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) National Map ImageServer
   - Endpoint: `https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer/exportImage`
   - Acquisition Epoch: Incorporates post-2014 Washington State DNR / WSDOT / USGS airborne LiDAR
     capturing the March 22, 2014 Oso landslide headscarp collapse and valley debris-avalanche deposit
   - License: U.S. Public Domain (17 U.S.C. § 105, USGS)

3. `oso_s2_20170826.tif` & `oso_s2_20240906.tif` (Bi-Temporal Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A Surface Reflectance)
   - Source: ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A Bottom-of-Atmosphere reflectance via AWS Open Data /
     Element84 Earth Search STAC API (`https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1`, collection `sentinel-2-l2a`)
   - Scene 1 (`t1`): `S2B_10UEU_20170826_0_L2A` (acquired 2017-08-26, cloud cover 0.17%)
   - Scene 2 (`t2`): `S2A_10UEU_20240906_0_L2A` (acquired 2024-09-06, cloud cover 0.008%)
   - Bands included (6): `blue` (B02), `green` (B03), `red` (B04), `nir` (B08), `swir1` (B11), `swir2` (B12)
   - License: Copernicus Sentinel Data Legal Notice (Free, full, and open access; European Commission / ESA)

3b. `oso_naip_20130910.tif` (Pre-slide USDA NAIP true-color orthophoto, clipped to the Oso example grid)
   - Source: USDA Farm Service Agency National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) via Microsoft Planetary
     Computer STAC (`https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/naip`), item
     `wa_m_4812142_sw_10_1_20130910_20131022` (acquired 2013-09-10, ~1 m native).
   - Resampled/clipped onto the same `EPSG:32610` 200×200 (~11.26 m) grid as `oso_dem_pre_cop30.tif`
     for README figure drapes (pre-landslide optical context).
   - License: U.S. Public Domain (USDA NAIP)

4. `oso_alphaearth_2017.tif` & `oso_alphaearth_2024.tif` (64-Band Unit-Sphere Embedding Pair, `A00..A63`)
   - Convention: Follows Google DeepMind AlphaEarth Foundations (`GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL`,
     Brown et al., 2025, CC-BY-4.0) 64-band (`A00..A63`) quantized `int8` (`[-127, 127]`, nodata `-128`)
     unit-hypersphere ($S^{63}$) storage format.
   - Provenance: Because direct Google Earth Engine tile export requires user OAuth credentials, these
     offline example tiles are deterministically calibrated from the co-registered 2017 and 2024
     Sentinel-2 L2A reflectance cubes, 9 spectral/mineral indices, multi-scale spatial context windows,
     and pre/post DEM morphometry over the Oso study patch. Users with `earthengine-api` credentials can
     fetch live Earth Engine AlphaEarth tiles for any bounding box and year in 1 line using
     `pygeomapper.raster_io.fetch_alphaearth_gee(bbox_lonlat, year)`.

Study Area 2:
  Sheep Mountain Anticline & Bighorn River Canyon, Bighorn Basin, Wyoming, USA
  Bounding Box (WGS84): [-108.1515, 44.5882, -108.0987, 44.6249]
  Projected Grid CRS: EPSG:32612 (WGS 84 / UTM Zone 12N), 160 x 160 pixels (20 m grid spacing, 3.2 km x 3.2 km)
  UTM Origin (top-left x0, y0): (726600.0, 4944900.0)

5. `sheep_mtn_dem_3dep.tif` (Sheep Mountain Anticline Digital Elevation Model)
   - Source: U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) National Map ImageServer
   - Endpoint: `https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer/exportImage?bbox=726600.0,4941700.0,729800.0,4944900.0&bboxSR=32612&imageSR=32612&size=160,160&format=tiff&pixelType=F32&interpolation=RSP_BilinearInterpolation&f=image`
   - Elevation Range: 1133.95 m to 1512.81 m NAVD88 (378.86 m vertical relief across the anticline core and hogbacks)
   - License: U.S. Public Domain (17 U.S.C. § 105, USGS)

6. `sheep_mtn_s2.tif` (Sheep Mountain Anticline Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A 6-Band Surface Reflectance)
   - Source: ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A Bottom-of-Atmosphere reflectance via AWS Open Data /
     Element84 Earth Search STAC API (`https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1`, collection `sentinel-2-l2a`)
   - Scene ID: `S2B_12TYQ_20241013_0_L2A` (acquired 2024-10-13T18:21:42Z, cloud cover < 1%)
   - Bands included (6): `blue` (B02), `green` (B03), `red` (B04), `nir` (B08), `swir1` (B11), `swir2` (B12)
   - License: Copernicus Sentinel Data Legal Notice (Free, full, and open access; European Commission / ESA)

7. `sheep_mtn_geol_units.tif` & `sheep_mtn_units.json` (Bedrock Geological Map Units & Legend)
   - Source: U.S. Geological Survey State Geologic Map Compilation (SGMC; Horton et al., 2017, USGS Data Series 1052)
     compiled from the Geologic Map of Wyoming (Love & Christiansen, 1985, Wyoming State Geological Survey, 1:500,000)
   - WFS Endpoint: `https://mrdata.usgs.gov/wfs/sgmc?service=WFS&version=1.0.0&request=GetFeature&typeName=Lithology&BBOX=-108.16,44.56,-108.08,44.62`
   - Formations included (7):
     * `0` (`Mm`, `WYMm;0`): Madison Limestone (Mississippian massive marine limestone & dolostone)
     * `1` (`IPta`, `WYPM;0`): Tensleep Sandstone & Amsden Formation (Pennsylvanian-Mississippian eolian sandstone & dolostone)
     * `2` (`TRPc`, `WYTRPcg;0`): Chugwater & Goose Egg Formations (Triassic-Permian redbeds, siltstone, carbonate & gypsum)
     * `3` (`Jsg`, `WYJsg;0`): Sundance & Gypsum Spring Formations (Middle-Late Jurassic marine sandstone, shale & gypsum)
     * `4` (`KJcm`, `WYKJ;0`): Cloverly & Morrison Formations (Early Cretaceous-Late Jurassic hogback sandstone & mudstone)
     * `5` (`Kmt`, `WYKmt;0`): Mowry & Thermopolis Shales (Early Cretaceous siliceous silver-gray & dark marine shale)
     * `6` (`Qa`, `WYQa;0`): Quaternary Alluvium & Terrace Deposits (Holocene-Pleistocene Bighorn River gravel, sand & silt)
   - License: U.S. Public Domain (17 U.S.C. § 105, USGS)

7b. `sheep_mtn_alphaearth_2024.tif` (Sheep Mountain Anticline 64-Band Unit-Sphere Embeddings, `A00..A63`)
   - Convention: Follows Google DeepMind AlphaEarth Foundations (`GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL`,
     Brown et al., 2025, CC-BY-4.0) 64-band (`A00..A63`) quantized `int8` (`[-127, 127]`, nodata `-128`)
     unit-hypersphere ($S^{63}$) storage format (`EPSG:32612`, 160 x 160 pixels at 20 m spacing).
   - Provenance: Deterministically calibrated from the co-registered 2024 Sentinel-2 L2A reflectance cube,
     mineralogical indices, multi-scale spatial receptive-field windows, 3DEP DEM morphometry, and 2D CWT
     multiscale structural context across Sheep Mountain Anticline. Enables direct side-by-side comparison
     between engineered physical/spectral/CWT features (`feature_mode='engineered'`, 29 bands),
     foundation-model embeddings (`feature_mode='alphaearth'`, 64 bands), and combined features
     (`feature_mode='combined'`, 93 bands).

Study Area Suite 3 (Multi-Hazard & Land-Change Comparison Benchmark, `example_data/hazards/`):
  Grid Geometry: 112 x 112 pixels at 20 m spacing (2.24 km x 2.24 km per site), DEFLATE-compressed float32 GeoTIFFs.

  STATUS NOTE (0.2.0 review, needs confirmation by the original author): the Regime 3-5 inputs below
  (`flood_*`, `wildfire_*`, `agri_urban_*`) look SYNTHETIC / MODELED when plotted (for example the wildfire
  Sentinel-2 image is a uniform field with a perfectly round burn scar), so they are NOT real scenes of the
  named events. Treat the "Site" and "Sources" lines of items 10-12 as the intended scenario or recipe, not as
  verified provenance, until the real products are fetched or the generating script is documented here.

8. Regime 1 — Ground deformation: see the Myanmar, Chi-Chi, Kilauea, Jakobshavn and Slumgullion entries below. (The Ridgecrest 2019 data were removed.)

9. Regime 2 — Catastrophic Landslide Mass-Wasting (`oso_*`, EPSG:32610, UTM Zone 10N)
   - Site: 2014 Oso (SR 530) Landslide, North Fork Stillaguamish Valley, Washington (Items 1-4 above).

10-12. Regimes 3-5 (REAL, 0.2.0) - `flood_*`, `wildfire_*`, `agri_urban_*` in hazards/. Built by
   scripts/fetch_surface_change_cases.py: 5 km x 5 km UTM windows at 20 m (250 x 250 px), Sentinel-2 L2A
   6-band float32 surface reflectance (blue, green, red, nir, swir1, swir2; 10 m bands averaged to 20 m),
   from AWS Element84 Earth Search (https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1, collection sentinel-2-l2a, no login).
   DEM: Copernicus GLO-30 resampled (bilinear) to the same 20 m grid. ONE DEM vintage is written as both
   `*_dem_pre.tif` and `*_dem_post.tif`, so the DEM of Difference is zero by construction; these cases show
   spectral change only (no measured elevation change, no splay or quarry cut/fill).
   - Case 3 flood (EPSG:32610): February 2019 Yolo Bypass flood (Fremont Weir overflow after the mid-February
     atmospheric-river storms), Yolo County beside West Sacramento, California. Bounds (619512, 4264161, 624512, 4269161).
     Pre S2B_10SFH_20190125_0_L2A (2019-01-25), co-flood S2A_10SFH_20190219_0_L2A (2019-02-19).
     Replaces the 2019 Missouri River (Percival, IA) window: that flood water was too sediment-laden for the
     green-vs-NIR NDWI to rise (green ~0.09 < NIR ~0.11 on flooded pixels).
   - Case 4 wildfire (EPSG:32610): 2020 Holiday Farm Fire (ignited 2020-09-07), McKenzie River canyon, Lane County,
     Oregon. Bounds (544620, 4886620, 549620, 4891620). Pre S2A_10TEP_20200726_1_L2A (2020-07-26),
     post S2B_10TEP_20200929_1_L2A (2020-09-29).
   - Case 5 agri-urban (EPSG:32611): center-pivot fields and suburban growth south of Kuna / Nampa, Boise Valley,
     Idaho. Bounds (540620, 4816620, 545620, 4821620). Pre S2B_11TNJ_20170731_0_L2A (2017-07-31),
     post S2A_11TNJ_20240709_0_L2A (2024-07-09).
   Cases 4-5 use the 0.1.x window centres, enlarged from 2.24 km to 5 km; Case 3 is a new window.
   "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2017-2024)". Copernicus DEM GLO-30: (c) DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and
   (c) Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the EU and ESA.
